# Candidate profile (fictional example)

This profile is invented for the demo. It exists so the advisor has someone to match against out of the box. To use your own, put `cv.pdf` and `operating-manual.pdf` in the project root and run `python -m src.cli profile refresh`, which writes a private, gitignored `candidate/profile.md` that takes precedence over this file.

## Operating manual

### Works well

- Entering an existing, complex system, building a working model of it, and deciding what to keep, fix or replace.
- End-to-end ownership of a problem area without managing people.
- Data-heavy product surfaces where correctness matters: money, reporting, permissions.
- Autonomy with a clear definition of done; written, asynchronous communication.

### Drains

- Ticket-queue execution without product context.
- Meeting-heavy days and rigid schedules.
- Exhaustive manual QA or pixel-level visual polish as the main job.
- Long-running on-call rotations and routine operational hygiene.
- Selling and rate negotiation.

### Conditions

- Remote, working EU hours. Up to three hours of overlap with US Eastern is fine; overnight schedules are not.
- Track A (stable role): core range 90,000 to 130,000 per year; below 90,000 only for unusual career capital.
- Track B (bridge contracts): floor of 60 USD per hour; 80 to 100 USD per hour for strong-fit work.

### Career capital priorities

- More React and Next.js production repetitions.
- Backend and PostgreSQL depth.
- Production AI features with verification guardrails.
- Credibility in the payments or health domain.
